# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Campus2Career

## Objective
Short launch-style brag video for Campus2Career, the Virtual Placement Assistant for NMIMS Hyderabad students.

## Output
- Composition: `brag-output/composition/` · Render: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape 1920x1080 · Duration: 20s

## Source Material
- Primary files read: README.md, index.html, src/index.css tokens, docs/screenshots (login, student portal), src/pages/*
- Tagline: "Your intelligent academic and career companion — built exclusively for NMIMS Hyderabad students."
- UI recreated: login split screen, student dashboard (goal chip, crimson banner, four stat cards), AI Skill Gap card, Placement Prep sidebar
- Verbatim copy: "Virtual Placement Assistant", "Campus2Career Portal — Live", "Placement Tracking / AI Insights / Career Roadmap / LeetCode Analytics", "Goal: Full-Stack Developer", "AI Skill Gap Analysis — Compare your profile against Full-Stack Developer benchmarks.", "View Analysis →", "6 distinct role portals" (README)
- Stand-ins: student "Aarav" and the stat values are invented placeholders.

## Creative Direction
- Tone: app-store; campus launch film in NMIMS crimson
- Hook: "Placement season. Where do you stand?" · Outro: Campus2Career + "Built exclusively for NMIMS Hyderabad students."
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, abstract filler

## Visual Identity
Crimson hsl(348,83%,47%) -> hsl(348,83%,35%), ink hsl(222,47%,11%), bg hsl(210,20%,98%), Inter 500-900 (local files in assets/fonts).

## Audio
- Role: warm bed + sparse motion-matched accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM) at 0.34, fade in 1.2s, fade out 17.5-20s
- Cue guidance: bundled preset; outro name beat-locked at 17.02s; stat cards on beats 7.02/7.52/8.02/8.52
- SFX: click (sign in, View Analysis), card-place (stat cards), drop (chips/hook), soft impact (headline), bell (name)
