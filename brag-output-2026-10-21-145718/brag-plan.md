# Brag Plan v2: Campus2Career — Virtual Placement Assistant (30s)

Length note: the user explicitly asked for 30 seconds, so this run goes past the skill's usual 15–25s window on purpose. Density per beat is kept the same as v1; the extra 10s goes to two more working-app scenes.

## What is this app?
A dual-portal AI career and placement platform built exclusively for NMIMS Hyderabad students, plus six role-based admin portals.

## The angle
Placement season as a confident campus launch film in NMIMS crimson. v2 shows the working app end to end: sign in, dashboard, skill gap, resume ATS match, algorithmic roadmap, then the six portals.

## Hook (0-3s)
"Placement season." slams in word by word, then "Where do you stand?"

## What changed vs v1 ("make it better")
- +10s: new Resume Analyzer scene (typed resume and job description, "Comparing Ontology...", ATS Match Probability ring counting up) and new roadmap scene (Build Fundamentals → Level Up to Intermediate → Master Advanced Patterns).
- Audio-reactive glow: music bass/treble drives the hook glow, banner shadow and outro glow (no waveform visuals).
- Fixed overlapping chips on the login scene; wipes alternate direction; word-by-word hook.
- Outro name beat-locked to the 27.02s strong cue; stat cards and roadmap cards on consecutive beats.

## User flow worth showing
Entry (login) → key action (dashboard, View Analysis, paste resume + JD) → result (skill gap, ATS match, roadmap).

## Tone
- Preset: app-store · Creative direction: campus launch film in NMIMS crimson, confident and clean
- Transitions: crimson wipes (0.3s) alternating direction
